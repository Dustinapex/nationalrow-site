While you wait — three things that help your case

None of this is urgent, but every one of them is easier to do now than later:

  • Photograph everything. Fences, gates, entrances, outbuildings, irrigation, crops, timber, the whole frontage. Before any survey crew changes it.
  • Keep every piece of paper. Letters, envelopes, business cards, appraisals, maps. Note the date on anything that arrived without one.
  • Write down what was said. Every call or visit from a right-of-way agent — who, when, and what they told you. Memory fades; notes don't.
